I am a PhD scholar and Prime Minister’s Research Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras), affiliated with the Wadhwani School of Data Science and the Department of Biotechnology. I am a member of the BioSystems Engineering and Control group, advised by Prof. Nirav Pravinbhai Bhatt.

My research is on machine learning for biology and chemistry: multimodal AI, molecular language models, interpretable molecular representations, and rigorous benchmarks for biological tasks. Recent work includes Leak-CURBER, a leakage-controlled benchmark for enzymatic reaction tasks (NeurIPS 2026 Evaluations and Datasets Track), BiCA, a biomedical dense retriever (AAAI 2026, oral), functional-group-based interpretable property prediction (JCIM 2026), and NovoMolGen, a study of molecular language model pretraining done with collaborators at Mila. In 2025 I was a research intern with Prof. Sarath Chandar at Mila - Quebec AI Institute, working on goal-aware retrosynthesis planning.

I hold a BTech in Biotechnology from NIT Calicut, where I graduated as a gold medalist. I also contribute to the OpenFold open-source initiative, co-organize the GCLR workshop at AAAI, and enjoy teaching. I have served as a teaching assistant for several courses at IIT Madras.
